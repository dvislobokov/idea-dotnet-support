Reports lookups that cost on every frame in Update, FixedUpdate, LateUpdate and OnGUI of a MonoBehaviour: GetComponent and its kin, FindObjectOfType / FindAnyObjectByType, GameObject.Find and FindWithTag, SendMessage, AddComponent.

Look such things up once, in Awake or Start, and keep them in a field. For GetComponent<T>() of the component itself the quick-fix does that: a field private T _t;, _t = GetComponent<T>(); in Awake (created when there is none), and the field in place of the call.
